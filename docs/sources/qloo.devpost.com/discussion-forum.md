## Prize pool not motivating

Light Y, 2026-10-10:

Many strong projects are built for each competition now that AI has made coding accessible. With only three prizes, the $15K first prize is very high, and most participants have little chance of winning. It would have been even better if the same $25K pool were split something like $10K first, $6K second, $4K third, and five $1K honorable mentions, rewarding more teams. Google and Amazon hackathons often offer honorable mentions and smaller prizes in large numbers, which motivates more people to participate because there’s a real hope of winning something.
