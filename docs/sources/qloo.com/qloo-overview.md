## Give agents a heart

Teach agentic systems to go beyond instructions and truly understand the people they serve. When agents notice what people love, every moment feels thoughtful, personal, and a little more human.

### Anticipate needs

The agent reads context like timing, weather, and habits, then reaches out with something useful before you think to ask.

### Guide decisions

Instead of endless options, the agent offers confident picks with clear reasons rooted in your tastes, plus relevant alternatives.

### Feel more human

The agent sounds like a thoughtful friend, handling details and following through, so every interaction feels personal instead of transactional.

## Taste context

Turn on a few tastes and watch Qloo read them together, then predict what else the people who share them are likely to love.

### Predictive inference

Additional tastes and cultural qualities inferred from this combination:

> When they go out to eat or spend an evening, they favor cozy, romantic spots. In brands, they tend to prefer a calm, understated style. Their music taste likely runs to empowering artists, and they also admire those who break new ground. On screen, they may enjoy mind-bending films and metaphysical dramas, along with tales of parallel universes.

## Across the globe, down to the street

Qloo has data covering the planet and can differentiate consumer preferences to a precision of hundreds of feet.

### Geo Explorer

Pick a place and up to one or two taste signals. The heat is how strongly Qloo associates that combination with each part of the area, and the list beside it is the restaurants those signals rank highest.

## Industries and sectors

Taste crosses categories, so one ontology powers products in very different industries and sectors.

- Banking and Finance: Rewards, offers and partners chosen by what people love, not by transaction code
- CPG: Which products belong in which stores, and what to launch next
- Entertainment: Discovery across catalogs, from a single title outward
- Media: Planning and personalization by taste, with no cookies and no profiles
- Real Estate: Which tenants and concepts fit a place, before anything is built
- Retail: Assortment and merchandising that fit the neighborhood and the person
- Technology: Grounding for assistants and agents, and personalization on the device
- Travel and Hospitality: Recommendations that fit a traveler’s taste, from dining to destinations

## Intelligence without identity

### Privacy as a system boundary, not a policy layer

Qloo was built from the beginning around a simple premise: cultural relevance can be inferred from entities, relationships, and context without maintaining identity-level consumer profiles.

- **Anonymized learning**: Permitted model improvement occurs through anonymized taste arrays
- **No consumer identity graph**: Qloo models culture and preference, not individual identities
- **No direct identifiers required**: No names, emails, phone numbers, or device identifiers are needed
- **Stateless inference**: Each request is evaluated from the context supplied at that moment

### Ethical AI

Qloo maintains a bright architectural line between cultural intelligence and consumer identity.

Because inference is deterministic and built on structured relationships rather than black-box generation, every result can be explained: which entities, which attributes, which affinities. That is what makes it ethical AI in practice, not just in policy.
